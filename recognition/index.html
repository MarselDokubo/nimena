RECOGNITION ARCHITECTURE

Different achievements need different evidence.

NIMENA must approve the final programme names, criteria, nomination authority and decision panels before any call opens.

Professional medals

Exceptional technical, leadership, service or innovation achievements assessed against published criteria.

Institutional awards

Recognition for members, teams, chapters, projects or organisations making a verified contribution.

Fellowship

A senior professional recognition route with eligibility, sponsors, evidence and formal approval.

Scholarships & grants

Competitive support for learning, research, travel or professional development when funding is approved.

NOMINATION GOVERNANCE

Every decision should be explainable.

Recognition creates credibility only when conflicts, evidence and authority are properly controlled.

01

Publish call

State the category, criteria, deadline, evidence and who may nominate.

02

Check eligibility

Confirm membership or category rules and require complete declarations.

03

Independent review

Use approved reviewers, declared conflicts and documented scoring.

04

Authorised decision

Record the recommendation, approving authority and decision basis.

05

Publish & retain

Add verified recipients to the archive and preserve the audit history.

VERIFIED ARCHIVE
