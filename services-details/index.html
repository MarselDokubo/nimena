Membership

Join a professional community for marine engineers, naval architects, students, graduates, academics and aligned maritime professionals.

Home Services Membership

Membership Categories & Professional Pathways

Membership categories include professional membership and affiliate pathways such as Associate, Graduate and Student membership. Applications are reviewed through the Institution’s membership process and presented for the appropriate approval.

Members can benefit from professional networking, technical seminars and conferences, access to knowledge resources, leadership mentoring, professional recognition and opportunities to remain connected with developments in marine engineering and naval architecture.

Why join NIMENA?

  • 01. Professional Network: connect with experienced practitioners, academics, administrators and maritime industry stakeholders.

  • 02. Technical Development: benefit from seminars, conferences, publications, knowledge resources and continuous professional learning.

  • 03. Recognition & Mentorship: access mentoring, professional recognition and opportunities to contribute to NIMENA activities and leadership.

How membership works

step 1

Apply

Choose the appropriate category and submit a complete membership application with the required supporting documents.

step 2

Review

The membership process checks eligibility, documentation and the applicable professional requirements.

step 3

Council Consideration

Qualifying applications proceed through the Institution’s approval process in line with the relevant Council cycle.

Ready to apply? Download the membership application form, complete the required information and supporting documentation, and contact the NIMENA Secretariat if you need guidance on the most appropriate membership pathway.
