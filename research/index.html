02 / FROM MANUSCRIPT TO ISSUE

A clear publishing pathway

Authors work directly in Open Journal Systems, where every manuscript, revision, review and decision remains attached to its scholarly record.

01Prepare

Choose the journal and article section; review author, ethics, data and formatting policies.

02Submit in OJS

Add authors, affiliations, declarations, manuscript files, figures and supplementary material.

03Editorial screening

Editors confirm scope, completeness, originality and readiness for peer review.

04Peer review

Qualified reviewers provide confidential recommendations and constructive author feedback.

05Revision & decision

Authors respond in OJS; editors issue revision, acceptance, resubmission or rejection decisions.

06Production

Accepted work proceeds through copyediting, layout, proof approval and issue scheduling.

Submitting a manuscript does not guarantee acceptance. Conference acceptance, NIMENA membership or sponsorship must never bypass editorial screening and peer review.

04 / CONFERENCES & PROCEEDINGS

One route from registration to presentation.

The conference module will support delegates, authors, reviewers, speakers and organisers without mixing conference decisions with journal acceptance.

Delegate services

Registration, member and non-member pricing, payments, receipts, QR attendance, CPD and certificates.

Conference CFP

Tracks, abstracts, full papers, blind review, scoring, revisions, decisions and speaker confirmation.

Programme operations

Sessions, rooms, speakers, chairs, schedules, check-in and attendance evidence.

Proceedings

Abstract books or proceedings, with optional invitation to a separately reviewed OJS special issue.

Publication boundary: conference acceptance does not equal journal acceptance. Any invited manuscript must enter the appropriate OJS workflow as a new scholarly record.
