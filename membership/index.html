MEMBER VALUE

A professional relationship that grows with your career.

NIMENA membership should make it easier to learn, contribute, connect and demonstrate continuing professional engagement.

Professional standing

A recognised membership pathway with transparent grade, status and renewal records.

CPD & lifelong learning

Courses, webinars, attendance records and certificates connected to your member profile.

Technical knowledge

CURRENT APPLICATION PATHWAYS

Choose the route that matches your present stage.

The names below follow NIMENA's current membership application material. Final eligibility, fees and advancement rules remain subject to formal approval.

01

Corporate

Professional individual membership for suitably qualified and experienced practitioners.

02

Associate

An affiliate professional route for people contributing to the maritime field.

03

Graduate

A pathway for eligible engineering graduates building supervised experience.

04

Student

Early professional connection for students in relevant disciplines.

05

Corporate Firm

Institutional membership for eligible companies and organisations.

APPLICATION & RENEWAL

A clear journey from interest to good standing.

Applicants and members should always know what is required, who is reviewing it and what happens next.

01

Create an account

Verify your email and begin the appropriate membership application.

02

Submit evidence

Provide qualifications, experience, referees and required supporting documents.

03

Professional review

The Secretariat checks completeness and authorised reviewers assess eligibility.

04

Decision & payment

Approved applicants receive the correct fee, membership number and confirmation.

05

Maintain standing

Renew, record CPD, update your profile and apply for grade advancement when eligible.

COMMUNITY

Professional relationships beyond an application form.

Chapters, technical participation, student and young-professional activity, mentoring and volunteering turn membership into an active professional community.
