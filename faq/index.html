Home Community FAQ

NIMENA welcomes qualified marine engineers, naval architects, graduates, students and other eligible professionals whose work or study aligns with the Institution’s membership requirements.

Pathways include professional membership as well as affiliate categories such as Associate, Graduate and Student membership. The appropriate category depends on qualifications and professional experience.

Download the membership form, complete the required information and supporting documents, and submit through the NIMENA membership process for review and consideration.

Members gain access to professional networking, technical learning, conferences, knowledge resources, mentoring, recognition and opportunities to contribute to the maritime profession.

NIMENA supports conferences, seminars, workshops, technical sessions and other continuing professional development activities that help members strengthen competence and stay connected with developments in the maritime sector.
