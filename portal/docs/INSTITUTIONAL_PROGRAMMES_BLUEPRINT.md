# NIMENA Institutional Programmes Blueprint

## Purpose

This extension connects four professional programmes to the member portal and the public website without turning the membership system into an unstructured general-purpose form.

1. **Accreditation & Endorsement** — assessment of approved training providers, courses, laboratories and academic programmes.
2. **Innovation & Technology Transfer** — industry challenges, solutions, startup showcases, patents, investor engagement and commercialisation support.
3. **Professional Magazine** — accessible technical commentary, interviews, chapter stories, industry trends and lessons from incidents. It is editorial content, not a peer-reviewed journal.
4. **Industry Partnerships & Sponsorships** — technical collaboration, programme partnership, sponsorship and institutional alliance proposals.

## Public and protected boundary

| Capability | Public website | Portal |
|---|---|---|
| Explain scope, criteria and process | Yes | Summary |
| Publish recognised-provider directory | Yes, approved records only | Review source |
| Publish innovation challenges/showcases | Yes, after approval | Submission and screening |
| Publish magazine stories | Yes | Pitch and editorial screening |
| Publish partners and opportunities | Yes, with consent | Proposal, due diligence and approvals |
| Store evidence and decisions | No | Yes, protected |
| Track status and corrections | No | Yes |

## Production roles

- Applicant or member
- Programme officer
- Technical assessor or editorial reviewer
- Conflict-of-interest reviewer
- Finance officer, where fees apply
- Approval authority
- Publisher
- System administrator

No user should review or approve a record where a declared conflict exists.

## Shared lifecycle

`Draft → Submitted → Administrative screening → Evidence requested or accepted → Specialist review → Decision → Publication/recognition → Monitoring → Renewal/closure`

Each programme may add programme-specific states, but every transition must retain its actor, timestamp, reason and previous value.

## Programme requirements

### Accreditation & Endorsement

- Provider identity, legal status and authorised contact
- Programme/course/laboratory details
- Curriculum, instructors, facilities, equipment and quality controls
- Relevant regulator or academic approvals
- Desktop review and, where required, site assessment
- Technical panel recommendation and formal approval
- Scope, validity period, conditions, suspension and renewal
- Public directory entry generated only from an approved decision

The public label must be precise: NIMENA approval does not imply government or academic accreditation unless the relevant authority has granted it.

### Innovation & Technology Transfer

- Industry problem statement or proposed solution
- Technology-readiness level
- Intellectual-property ownership and disclosure
- Team, partners and existing funding
- Support requested: validation, pilot, mentoring, showcase, investor introduction or licensing
- Confidential and public summaries kept separately
- Conflict checks and commercialisation outcomes

Submission never transfers intellectual-property ownership to NIMENA without a separate signed agreement.

### Professional Magazine

- Pitch, audience and accessible synopsis
- Author biography and disclosure of interests
- Editorial fact-checking and technical review where required
- Copyright/licensing permission for text and media
- Corrections, withdrawal and publication history
- Clear separation from AJOMENA/JBESED peer review

### Industry Partnerships & Sponsorships

- Organisation profile and due-diligence evidence
- Shared objective and intended beneficiaries
- Cash or in-kind contribution
- Brand use, visibility and deliverables
- Agreement owner, start/end dates and reporting
- Ethical review, conflicts and restricted-sector rules
- Public acknowledgement only after agreement and consent

## Minimum production controls

- Verified accounts and role-based permissions
- Private object storage and expiring evidence links
- Malware and file-type checks
- Versioned criteria and decision templates
- Full audit log
- Consent, privacy notice and retention schedule
- Accessibility and mobile testing
- Backups and restoration tests
- Separate production, staging and preview environments

The prototype intentionally contains no real approvals and publishes no provider, partner or innovation claim.
