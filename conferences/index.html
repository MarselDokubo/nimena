CONFERENCE CALL FOR PAPERS

A review route designed for programmes and proceedings.

Authors can submit an abstract or full paper to an approved conference track. Review, decision, revision and scheduling remain separate from journal acceptance.

  • Track, topic, format and deadline selection
  • Blind review and conflict-of-interest declarations
  • Scored recommendations and authorised decisions
  • Speaker profile, programme scheduling and presentation status
  • Proceedings or optional invitation to a journal special issue
