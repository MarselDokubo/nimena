NIMENA at Singapore Maritime Week

During Singapore Maritime Week 2026, NIMENA participated in the Plug and Play x NIMENA Innovation Night, bringing Nigerian maritime engineering perspectives into conversations on innovation, technical sovereignty, local capacity and international collaboration.

Home Community Professional Activities

Connecting Nigerian maritime expertise with global innovation.

The engagement connected NIMENA with maritime leaders, investors, policymakers, academics and technology stakeholders, creating space for practical conversations on scaling Nigeria’s blue economy and strengthening indigenous engineering capability.

NIMENA’s message centred on building local technical capability, stronger standards, research-to-industry pathways and collaboration among government, industry and academia.

Beyond formal technical sessions, NIMENA activities strengthen professional relationships, expose younger professionals to experienced practitioners and create opportunities for collaboration across academia, industry and public institutions.

International Engagement in Practice

The Institution’s activity programme is designed to keep the professional community informed, connected and engaged with the technical and policy issues shaping marine engineering and naval architecture in Nigeria.

01. Purpose

Create meaningful platforms for knowledge exchange, professional development, peer learning and technical dialogue across the marine and maritime engineering community.

02. Sector Impact

Connect professional expertise with industry priorities so that engineering knowledge contributes to safer operations, stronger local capacity, innovation and sustainable maritime development.
